## 📊 Sector Overview & Compliance Dynamics

Modern compliance has evolved from static annual spreadsheets and manual audit binders into continuous compliance monitoring, automated evidence gathering, and code-driven policy enforcement:

- **Continuous Compliance & Audit Automation:** Cloud-native platforms continuously monitor AWS, GCP, Azure, HRIS, and identity systems to maintain compliance readiness for SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and PCI DSS.
- **Enterprise GRC Platforms:** Heavyweight suites manage enterprise risk management (ERM), policy lifecycle management, internal audit tracking, and vendor risk assessments across regulated verticals.
- **Policy-as-Code & Open-Source Security Automation:** Teams define compliance baselines directly within infrastructure-as-code and container pipelines using declarative policies, CIS benchmark scanners, and open GRC engines.

## 🛠️ How to Contribute

Contributions are warmly welcomed! Please read these quick guidelines before submitting:

1. **Check for duplicates** – ensure the suggested tool or platform isn't already included.
2. **Open-Source criteria** – provide the repository link and brief description highlighting compliance capabilities.
3. **Commercial SaaS criteria** – include transparent pricing and free tier/trial notes in accordance with community formatting.
4. **Submit a clean PR** – maintain alphabetical or star-sorted ordering in the respective section.
